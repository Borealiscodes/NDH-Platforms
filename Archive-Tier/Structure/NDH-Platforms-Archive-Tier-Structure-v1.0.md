This document defines the structure, purpose, and rules of the Archive Tier

The Archive Tier contains expressive artifacts that:

- Are expressive lineage  
- Are no longer active in the Emergent Tier  
- Are not runtime or governance artifacts  
- Are not hybrid manifolds  
- Require preservation for cosmology continuity  

This tier is the historical memory of NDH expressive cosmology.
