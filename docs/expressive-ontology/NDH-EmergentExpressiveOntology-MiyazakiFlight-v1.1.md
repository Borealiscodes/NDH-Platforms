# 1. **Narrative Context (Expressive Seed)**

Miyazaki’s films treat flight as a moral, emotional, and ontological space.  
Aircraft are not machines; they are characters.  
The sky is not background; it is a moral manifold.

His expressive narrative includes:

- Planes behaving like living creatures  
- Flight as freedom, but also responsibility  
- The sky as a place where intention becomes visible  
- Beauty entangled with consequence  
- Machines amplifying human intention without absorbing it  

This narrative is the expressive seed from which the ontology emerges.
