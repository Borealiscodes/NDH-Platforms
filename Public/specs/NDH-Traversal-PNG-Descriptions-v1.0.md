# **NDH Traversal PNG Descriptions v1.0**  
### *Canonical specifications for the four traversal PNG assets*

---

## 🟢 **Safety Envelope Overview**  
### **Filename**  
```
docs/public/img/safety-envelope-overview.png
```

### **Purpose**  
Visualizes the NDH Safety Envelope boundary and the gentle entry gate.  
Provides emotional grounding before traversal begins.

### **Required Elements**  
- **Soft boundary shape**  
  - Oval or circular  
  - Low‑contrast pastel gradient  
  - No sharp edges  
- **Entry Gate**  
  - Rounded rectangle  
  - Soft glow  
  - Gentle arrow pointing inward  
- **Guardian Presence (optional)**  
  - Tiny Snek near the boundary  
  - Neutral posture (not warning)  
- **Interior Space**  
  - Light, calm background  
  - No clutter  
  - No directional arrows (movement belongs to traversal PNGs)

### **Emotional Constraints**  
- Must feel safe, calm, and welcoming  
- No urgency, no tension  
- No metaphor drift  
- No animation or high contrast  

---

## 🟢 **Orbital Rings**  
### **Filename**  
```
docs/public/img/orbital-rings.png
```

### **Purpose**  
Expressive depiction of the three traversal orbits:  
Shallow → Medium → Deep.

### **Required Elements**  
- **Three concentric rings**  
  - Outer ring: Shallow Orbit  
  - Middle ring: Medium Orbit  
  - Inner ring: Deep Orbit  
- **Color gradient**  
  - Soft transition from outer → inner  
  - Pastel palette  
- **Tiny Sneks marking transitions**  
  - Positioned between rings  
  - Neutral or guiding posture  
- **Movement arrows**  
  - Only allowed transitions: S→M→D and D→M→S  
  - Arrows must be soft, rounded, low‑contrast  
- **Labels**  
  - “Shallow Orbit”  
  - “Medium Orbit”  
  - “Deep Orbit”  
  - Typography must be gentle and readable

### **Emotional Constraints**  
- No urgency  
- No recursion arrows  
- No “danger” indicators  
- Must visually reinforce pacing and safety  

---

## 🟢 **Guardian Roles**  
### **Filename**  
```
docs/public/img/snek-guardian-roles.png
```

### **Purpose**  
Shows the three guardian actions:  
Warn → Redirect → Soften.

### **Required Elements**  
- **Three Sneks**  
  - Each representing one guardian role  
  - Gentle Kawaii style  
  - No aggressive posture  
- **Color coding**  
  - Warn: soft yellow  
  - Redirect: soft blue  
  - Soften: soft green  
- **Rounded icons**  
  - Warn: ⚠️ (soft outline)  
  - Redirect: ↩️ (rounded arrow)  
  - Soften: 🌿 (leaf or sprout)  
- **Layout**  
  - Horizontal or vertical triptych  
  - Equal spacing  
  - No clutter  

### **Emotional Constraints**  
- Sneks must appear supportive, not corrective  
- No red, no sharp triangles, no exclamation marks  
- No directional arrows except the Redirect icon  

---

## 🟢 **Trans‑Orbital Flow**  
### **Filename**  
```
docs/public/img/trans-orbital-flow.png
```

### **Purpose**  
Shows the traversal pipeline:  
User → Entry Gate → Orbit Selector → Depth/Pace → Guardian → Soft Exit.

### **Required Elements**  
- **Five rounded nodes**  
  - “User Intent”  
  - “Entry Gate”  
  - “Orbital Selector”  
  - “Depth & Pace Controller”  
  - “Guardian Intervention”  
  - “Soft Exit”  
- **Soft arrows**  
  - Rounded, low‑contrast  
  - No branching  
  - No recursion arrows  
- **Optional Snek**  
  - Near Guardian node  
  - Gentle posture  
- **Color palette**  
  - Pastel blues, greens, purples  
  - No high‑contrast transitions  
- **Flow direction**  
  - Left → right  
  - Or top → bottom  
  - Must be linear and calm  

### **Emotional Constraints**  
- No urgency  
- No “danger” colors  
- No sharp directional cues  
- Must visually reinforce pacing and safety
