# **🎨 NDH Render Spec — Habitat Map PNG v1.0**  
### *NDH‑Platforms → Public → specs*

A trauma‑informed rendering specification defining how the **Habitat Map PNG** must be constructed, governed, and integrated into the NDH public expressive layer.

The Habitat Map is the **public‑facing geography** of NDH — a gentle metaphorical landscape that helps users understand NDH’s conceptual terrain without invoking depth traversal or emotional escalation.

---

## **1 — Purpose & Ontology Binding**

### **Purpose**
The Habitat Map PNG provides a **calm, metaphorical overview** of NDH’s public conceptual regions:

- Precursor Thicket  
- Commit Forest  
- Governance Cave  
- Manifold River  

It must feel:

- gentle  
- safe  
- non‑directive  
- non‑hierarchical  
- non‑evaluative  

### **Ontology Bindings**
- **Public Ontology Categories**  
- **Guardian Roles** (ambient presence only)  
- **Safety Envelope** (outer boundary)  
- **Expressive Metaphor Layer**  

---

## **2 — Canvas Specification**

### **Canvas Size**
- **1800 × 1000 px**  
- Transparent background  
- Landscape orientation  

### **Canvas Zones**
- **Top‑Left:** Precursor Thicket  
- **Center‑Left:** Commit Forest  
- **Center‑Right:** Governance Cave  
- **Bottom‑Right:** Manifold River  

Zones must be visually balanced and emotionally neutral.

---

## **3 — Palette Specification**

### **Primary Colors (NDH Pastel Set)**
- Thicket Green (#DFF5D1)  
- Forest Green (#C8EFC4)  
- Cave Gray (#E6E6E6)  
- River Blue (#CDE7FF)  
- Safety Lavender (#CFC7FF) — outer boundary  

### **Rules**
- No saturated greens or blues  
- No dark cave tones  
- No high‑contrast edges  
- No urgency colors  
- No metaphorically “dangerous” palettes  

Palette must follow **Blueprint Suite v1.0**.

---

## **4 — Geometry Specification**

### **Shapes**
- Organic rounded shapes  
- No sharp corners  
- No jagged edges  
- No literal terrain (no cliffs, spikes, or steep slopes)  

### **Zone Geometry**
- **Thicket:** clustered rounded shapes  
- **Forest:** larger rounded shapes with gentle spacing  
- **Cave:** soft oval or rounded hollow  
- **River:** wide, gentle curve  

### **Line Weight**
- 3–5 px  
- Soft opacity (70–85%)

---

## **5 — Trauma‑Informed Constraints**

### **Required**
- No danger metaphors  
- No steep slopes  
- No dark caves  
- No “lost” symbolism  
- No urgency cues  
- No directional arrows  
- No forced movement  

### **Emotional Tone**
- Calm  
- Exploratory  
- Non‑directive  
- Gentle  
- Safe  

### **Guardian Posture**
- Guardians may appear as **tiny ambient silhouettes**, not active figures.  
- They must not imply evaluation or direction.

---

## **6 — Layout Specification**

### **Precursor Thicket**
- Soft clustered shapes  
- Light green palette  
- Label: “Precursor Thicket”  
- Represents early ideas, seedlings, beginnings  

### **Commit Forest**
- Larger rounded shapes  
- Medium green palette  
- Label: “Commit Forest”  
- Represents stable, grounded work  

### **Governance Cave**
- Soft gray oval  
- Slight inner glow (5–10%)  
- Label: “Governance Cave”  
- Represents reflection, structure, and safety  

### **Manifold River**
- Gentle blue curve  
- No sharp bends  
- Label: “Manifold River”  
- Represents flow, connection, and expressive motion  

### **Safety Envelope Boundary**
- Soft lavender perimeter  
- Very low opacity glow (10–15%)  
- Must feel protective, not restrictive  

---

## **7 — Labeling Specification**

### **Required Labels**
- “Precursor Thicket”  
- “Commit Forest”  
- “Governance Cave”  
- “Manifold River”  
- “Safety Envelope”  

### **Font**
- Rounded sans‑serif  
- 24–32 px  
- Soft gray (#6A6A6A)  
- No bold  
- No italics  

### **Placement**
- Centered within each zone  
- Never touching edges  
- Never overlapping shapes  

---

## **8 — Reversibility Specification**

The PNG must visually imply:

- Users can explore any region without commitment  
- Movement is optional  
- No region is “better” or “higher”  
- The map is a landscape, not a progression  
- The Safety Envelope surrounds everything gently  

Reversibility is a **core NDH trauma‑informed invariant**.

---

## **9 — Integration Rules**

### **Public UX**
- Used in onboarding  
- Used in public documentation  
- Used in expressive NDH explanations  

### **Blueprint Suite**
- Must follow palette, geometry, trauma‑informed rules  

### **PNG Integration Blueprint**
- Must bind to ontology categories  
- Must bind to expressive metaphor layer  
- Must bind to safety envelope semantics  

### **ASCII/SVG Alignment**
- ASCII remains canonical  
- SVG remains structural  
- PNG remains expressive
