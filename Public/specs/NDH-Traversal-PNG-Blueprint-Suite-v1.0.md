# **NDH Traversal PNG Blueprint Suite v1.0**  
### *Global Rendering Doctrine for All Traversal PNGs*

---

## 🌿 **1 — Purpose of the Blueprint Suite**
The Blueprint Suite defines the **global rules** that every traversal PNG must follow.

It ensures:

- emotional consistency  
- visual consistency  
- trauma‑informed safety  
- palette stability  
- geometry stability  
- metaphor stability  
- NDH governance compliance  

It is the **umbrella specification** for:

- **Safety Envelope Overview**  
- **Orbital Rings**  
- **Guardian Roles**  
- **Trans‑Orbital Flow**  

---

## 🌿 **2 — Global Trauma‑Informed Constraints**
All traversal PNGs must follow NDH trauma‑informed design rules:

- No sharp edges  
- No high contrast  
- No red  
- No urgency cues  
- No flashing or animation  
- No metaphor drift  
- No clutter  
- No aggressive posture  
- No branching arrows  
- No recursion arrows  

Emotional tone must be:

- calm  
- gentle  
- predictable  
- safe  
- welcoming  

---

## 🌿 **4 — Global Geometry Rules**
All traversal PNGs must follow these geometry constraints:

### **4.1 Rounded Everything**
- Rounded rectangles  
- Rounded arrows  
- Rounded triangles  
- Rounded nodes  
- Rounded rings  

### **4.2 Stroke Rules**
- Standard stroke: **4px**  
- Secondary stroke: **2px**  
- Arrow stroke: **3px**  
- Ring stroke: **6px**

### **4.3 Glow Rules**
- Outer glow: **8px blur**, 20% opacity  
- Glow color must match the element’s hue  

### **4.4 Arrow Rules**
- No sharp arrowheads  
- No branching  
- No recursion  
- No urgency cues  
- Only forward movement  

---

## 🌿 **5 — Global Snek Posture Rules**
Sneks must always appear:

- gentle  
- supportive  
- non‑aggressive  
- non‑threatening  
- emotionally neutral or calming  

Allowed postures:

- curled  
- resting  
- guiding  
- observing  

Forbidden postures:

- raised head  
- open mouth  
- sharp teeth  
- aggressive coil  

Sneks must be **small**, **soft**, and **optional**.

---

## 🌿 **6 — Global Typography Rules**
- Font: **Inter Rounded**  
- Weight: Regular  
- Color: **#6A6A6A**  
- No bold  
- No italics  
- No uppercase shouting  
- No red text  

Typography must be gentle and readable.

---

## 🌿 **7 — Global Canvas Rules**
All traversal PNGs must use:

- **White or near‑white backgrounds** (#FAFAFA)  
- **No texture**  
- **No noise**  
- **No gradients except interior soft fills**  

Canvas sizes:

| PNG | Size |
|-----|------|
| Safety Envelope | 1600×1200 |
| Orbital Rings | 1600×1600 |
| Guardian Roles | 1800×600 |
| Trans‑Orbital Flow | 2000×1200 |

These sizes are **mandatory**.

---

## 🌿 **8 — Global Directory Rules**
All traversal PNGs must live under:

```
docs/public/img/
```

Filenames must be:

- lowercase  
- hyphenated  
- stable  
- descriptive  
- NDH‑aligned  

---

## 🌿 **9 — Global Rendering Pipeline**
Traversal PNGs must follow this pipeline:

1. **Blueprint Suite (this document)**  
2. **Individual Render Specs**  
3. **Rendering**  
4. **Governance Review**  
5. **Public Layer Integration**  
6. **Versioning**  

PNG rendering must **never** occur without a Render Spec.
