## 🟢 **5 — Emotional Constraints**
The PNG **must** follow NDH trauma‑informed rules:

- no sharp edges  
- no high contrast  
- no red  
- no urgency cues  
- no directional arrows except the gentle inward gate arrow  
- no metaphor drift  
- no animation  
- no clutter  

The Safety Envelope must feel:

- calm  
- protective  
- spacious  
- welcoming
