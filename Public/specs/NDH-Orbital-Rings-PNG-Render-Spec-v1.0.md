# **🟣 NDH Orbital Rings PNG Description v1.0**  
### *Canonical rendering specification for `orbital-rings.png`*

---

## 🟢 **Orbital Rings Overview**  
### **Filename**  
```
docs/public/img/orbital-rings.png
```

### **Purpose**  
Visualizes the **three traversal orbits** used in Trans‑Orbital Traversal:

- Shallow Orbit  
- Medium Orbit  
- Deep Orbit  

The PNG provides gentle emotional grounding for movement between conceptual depths.

---

## 🟢 **Canvas Geometry**  
- **Size:** 1600 × 1600 px  
- **Background:** transparent  
- **Style:** low‑stimulus, no texture, no noise  
- **Reason:** rings must float cleanly in UX contexts

---

## 🟢 **Ring Geometry**  
Three concentric rings, centered perfectly.

### **Outer Ring — Shallow Orbit**
- Stroke: 6px  
- Color: pastel blue (#CDE7FF)  
- Glow: 8px blur, 20% opacity  
- Emotion: gentle, accessible  

### **Middle Ring — Medium Orbit**
- Stroke: 6px  
- Color: pastel purple (#D9C8FF)  
- Glow: 8px blur, 20% opacity  
- Emotion: calm, deeper engagement  

### **Inner Ring — Deep Orbit**
- Stroke: 6px  
- Color: pastel pink (#FFD6E8)  
- Glow: 8px blur, 20% opacity  
- Emotion: introspective, safe depth  

All rings must be:

- perfectly circular  
- evenly spaced  
- softly rendered  
- non‑aggressive  

---

## 🟢 **Labels**  
Each ring must have a label:

- “Shallow Orbit”  
- “Medium Orbit”  
- “Deep Orbit”  

### Typography  
- Font: Inter Rounded  
- Color: #6A6A6A  
- Weight: Regular  
- No bold, no italics  
- Placement: near each ring, not touching the stroke  

---

## 🟢 **Movement Arrows**  
Arrows show **allowed traversal transitions**:

- Shallow → Medium  
- Medium → Deep  
- Deep → Medium  
- Medium → Shallow  

### Arrow Rules  
- Rounded arrowheads  
- Stroke: 3px  
- Color: soft gray (#BFBFBF)  
- No branching  
- No recursion  
- No urgency cues  
- No red  

Arrows must be gentle, curved slightly, and emotionally neutral.

---

## 🟢 **Transition Sneks**  
Tiny Sneks mark safe transition points.

### Snek Rules  
- Style: Kawaii, gentle  
- Color: soft purple (#BFA9FF)  
- Posture: curled or guiding  
- Placement: between rings  
- Emotion: supportive, not corrective  

Sneks must be optional but recommended.

---

## 🟢 **Emotional Constraints**  
The PNG must follow NDH trauma‑informed rules:

- no sharp edges  
- no high contrast  
- no red  
- no urgency cues  
- no aggressive posture  
- no clutter  
- no metaphor drift  

The overall tone must be:

- calm  
- predictable  
- safe  
- gently informative
