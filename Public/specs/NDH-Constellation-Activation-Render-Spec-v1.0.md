# **🌌 NDH Constellation Activation PNG Render Spec v1.0**  
### *NDH‑Platforms → Public → specs*

A trauma‑informed rendering specification defining how the **Constellation Activation PNG** must be constructed, governed, and integrated into the NDH expressive layer.  
This PNG visually represents the moment when all traversal and public PNGs form a unified constellation under the Safety Envelope.

---

## **1 — Purpose & Ontology Binding**

### **Purpose**
The Constellation Activation PNG provides a **gentle, non‑hierarchical visualization** of:

- traversal stars  
- public stars  
- guardian semantics  
- safety envelope glow  
- reversible binding lines  

It must feel:

- calm  
- expansive  
- non‑directive  
- non‑hierarchical  
- emotionally safe  

### **Ontology Bindings**
- **Traversal Nodes**  
- **Public Nodes**  
- **Guardian Roles**  
- **Safety Envelope**  
- **Constellation Activation Diagram**  

---

## **2 — Canvas Specification**

### **Canvas Size**
- **2000 × 1200 px**  
- Transparent background  
- Landscape orientation  

### **Canvas Zones**
- **Inner Ring:** Traversal stars  
- **Outer Ring:** Public stars  
- **Perimeter:** Safety Envelope glow  

Zones must be balanced and non‑hierarchical.

---

## **3 — Palette Specification**

### **Primary Colors (NDH Pastel Set)**
- Star White (#FFFFFF, 85% opacity)  
- Orbit Lavender (#CFC7FF)  
- Safety Glow (#EDE7FF)  
- Traversal Blue (#D7E9FF)  
- Public Green (#DFF5D1)  

### **Rules**
- No saturated colors  
- No sharp contrasts  
- No urgency colors  
- No dark voids or cosmic metaphors  
- No literal stars or astronomy symbolism  

This is a **conceptual constellation**, not a celestial one.

---

## **4 — Geometry Specification**

### **Shapes**
- Rounded star‑nodes (soft circles, 40–60 px)  
- Gentle arcs connecting nodes  
- No straight lines  
- No sharp corners  
- No literal star points  

### **Ring Geometry**
- **Inner Ring:** Traversal nodes arranged in a soft oval  
- **Outer Ring:** Public nodes arranged in a larger oval  
- **Safety Envelope:** Soft perimeter glow (15–20% opacity)

### **Line Weight**
- 3–4 px  
- 70–80% opacity  
- Soft pastel arcs  

---

## **5 — Trauma‑Informed Constraints**

### **Required**
- No hierarchy  
- No “center of importance”  
- No evaluative symbolism  
- No urgency cues  
- No directional arrows  
- No forced movement  
- No cosmic metaphors (no stars, galaxies, nebulae)

### **Emotional Tone**
- Gentle  
- Expansive  
- Reversible  
- Safe  
- Non‑directive  

### **Guardian Posture**
Guardians appear as **tiny ambient silhouettes**, not active figures.

---

## **6 — Layout Specification**

### **Traversal Inner Ring**
Nodes:
- Safety Envelope  
- Orbital Rings  
- Guardian Roles  
- Trans‑Orbital Flow  

Layout:
- Soft oval  
- Even spacing  
- No implied progression  

### **Public Outer Ring**
Nodes:
- Public Interaction Flow  
- Soft Mistake Handling  
- Habitat Map  

Layout:
- Larger oval  
- Even spacing  
- No implied hierarchy  

### **Binding Lines**
- Gentle arcs connecting inner → outer  
- No radial spokes  
- No center‑weighted geometry  

### **Safety Envelope Glow**
- Soft lavender perimeter  
- 10–15% opacity  
- Must feel protective, not restrictive  

---

## **7 — Labeling Specification**

### **Required Labels**
- “Traversal Ring”  
- “Public Ring”  
- Node names (7 total)  
- “Safety Envelope”  

### **Font**
- Rounded sans‑serif  
- 24–32 px  
- Soft gray (#6A6A6A)  
- No bold  
- No italics  

### **Placement**
- Centered near each node  
- Never touching arcs  
- Never overlapping rings  

---

## **8 — Reversibility Specification**

The PNG must visually imply:

- All nodes are equal  
- All paths are reversible  
- No node is central  
- No node is “higher”  
- No node is “first”  
- The constellation is a **field**, not a sequence  

Reversibility is a **core NDH invariant**.

---
