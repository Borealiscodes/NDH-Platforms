# **🟢 NDH Guardian Roles PNG Description v1.0**  
### *Canonical rendering specification for `snek-guardian-roles.png`*

---

## 🟢 **Guardian Roles Overview**  
### **Filename**  
```
docs/public/img/snek-guardian-roles.png
```

### **Purpose**  
Visualizes the **three Guardian actions** used throughout NDH traversal:

- Warn  
- Redirect  
- Soften  

These roles are emotional‑safety primitives, not enforcement mechanisms.  
The PNG must communicate **gentle support**, not correction.

---

## 🟢 **Canvas Geometry**  
- **Size:** 1800 × 600 px  
- **Background:** white (#FFFFFF)  
- **Layout:** three equal vertical panels  
- **Style:** low‑stimulus, no texture, no noise  

This layout ensures each Guardian role is visually distinct yet part of a unified system.

---

## 🟢 **Panel Structure**  
Each panel contains:

- a gentle Snek  
- a soft background color  
- a rounded icon  
- a label  

Panels must be evenly spaced with soft boundaries (no hard lines).

### Panel Colors  
- **Warn:** pastel yellow (#FFF4C2)  
- **Redirect:** pastel blue (#CDE7FF)  
- **Soften:** pastel green (#DFFFEA)

These colors are mandatory and come from the NDH Pastel Core Palette.

---

## 🟢 **Snek Geometry**  
Sneks must follow NDH posture rules:

- curled or gently upright  
- soft outlines  
- no sharp teeth  
- no aggressive coil  
- no raised head  
- no tension cues  

### Snek Color  
- soft purple (#BFA9FF)

### Snek Size  
- small, non‑dominant  
- centered within each panel  

Sneks must appear **supportive**, not corrective.

---

## 🟢 **Role Icons**  
Each role has a rounded icon:

- **Warn:** ⚠️ (rounded triangle, soft outline)  
- **Redirect:** ↩️ (rounded arrow, soft curve)  
- **Soften:** 🌿 (leaf or sprout, soft edges)

### Icon Rules  
- Color: medium gray (#6A6A6A)  
- Stroke: 2px  
- No red  
- No sharp angles  
- No urgency cues  

Icons must be gentle and emotionally neutral.

---

## 🟢 **Labels**  
Each panel must include a label:

- “Warn”  
- “Redirect”  
- “Soften”

### Typography  
- Font: Inter Rounded  
- Color: #5A5A5A  
- Weight: Regular  
- No bold  
- No uppercase  
- Placement: below icon, above Snek  

Labels must be calm and readable.

---

## 🟢 **Emotional Constraints**  
The PNG must follow NDH trauma‑informed rules:

- no sharp edges  
- no high contrast  
- no red  
- no urgency cues  
- no aggressive posture  
- no clutter  
- no metaphor drift  

The overall tone must be:

- calm  
- supportive  
- predictable  
- safe
