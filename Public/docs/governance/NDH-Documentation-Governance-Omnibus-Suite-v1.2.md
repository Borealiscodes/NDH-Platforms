# ⭐ 1 — Corrected Emergent Case Study  
### *Temporal Emergence, Structural Divergence, and Cross‑Layer Interaction*

The **NDH Documentation Governance Charter v1.1** predates the **SerenityBasis Unified Governance Omnibus v1.0** by **four days**.  
This temporal ordering establishes a **strict upstream/downstream relationship**:

- **Upstream:** Charter governs documentation rules  
- **Downstream:** Omnibus governs expressive geometry  

The Omnibus **inherits** Charter constraints, but the Charter **does not inherit** Omnibus geometry.

This upstream/downstream relationship is foundational to NDH governance and directly informs the Expansion Dilemma.

# ⭐ 2 — Integrated Expansion Dilemma (Public‑Facing)  
### *Why the Suite Required Expansion, and How NDH Maintains Stability Across Layers*

The Omnibus Suite occupies a **unique and delicate position** within the NDH ecosystem.  
It must explain how documentation remains stable, trauma‑informed, and accessible, while also clarifying how NDH’s internal governance artifacts maintain structural separation from documentation rules.

This creates a tension:

> **The Suite must be clear enough to prevent confusion,  
> but gentle enough to avoid overwhelming contributors.**

NDH governance faced a decision:

- **Expand the Suite now** to ensure cross‑layer coherence during active development  
- **or**  
- **Wait until baseline stabilization** to avoid premature governance commitments

### Why Expansion Was Necessary  
NDH is in an **active governance construction phase**, and several risks were emerging:

- documentation rules drifting into manifold geometry  
- expressive geometry rules leaking into documentation  
- trauma‑informed boundaries blurring  
- provenance lineage becoming unstable  
- future governance artifacts inheriting unclear boundaries  

To prevent these risks, NDH governance determined that **expansion now** was the safer path.

### Public‑Facing Rationale  
> **Expanding the Suite early protects contributors from unclear boundaries  
> and ensures NDH documentation remains gentle, predictable, and stable.**

### What Expansion Added  
The Suite now includes:

- a corrected emergent case study  
- a unified comparison spec  
- a Charter↔Omnibus crossmap  
- a visual governance diagram  
- expanded trauma‑informed boundaries  
- upstream/downstream clarification  
- membrane adjacency and gate‑state purity rules  
- semantic isolation between layers  

These additions are **documentation‑safe**, **public‑appropriate**, and **trauma‑informed**.

They do not expose internal manifold geometry.  
They do not introduce high‑stimulus content.  
They do not overwhelm contributors.
