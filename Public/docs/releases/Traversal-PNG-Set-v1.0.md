## 🧭 **Design Principles**
- **Transparent backgrounds** for accessibility and adaptive integration.  
- **Pastel palette** for emotional neutrality and calm tone.  
- **Rounded geometry** to avoid sharp edges and urgency cues.  
- **Consistent visual rhythm** across all traversal layers.  
- **Trauma‑informed composition** ensuring safety, clarity, and reversibility.
