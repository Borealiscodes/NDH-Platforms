# **📋 NDH Expressive Suite Publication Envelope Governance Risk Register v1.0**  
### *NDH‑Platforms → Public → governance*

The **Governance Risk Register** catalogues all known risks that could affect the stability, neutrality, reversibility, accessibility, or trauma‑informed integrity of the NDH Expressive Suite Publication Envelope.  
Each risk is mapped to its invariant, assessed for impact, and paired with governed mitigation pathways and continuity anchors.

This register is the **governance risk backbone** of the Publication Envelope.

---

## **1 — Purpose of the Risk Register**

The register:

- identifies all governance‑level risks  
- ensures trauma‑informed invariants remain protected  
- prevents architectural drift or accidental hierarchy  
- supports ELGG stewardship and audit cycles  
- provides continuity across version transitions  
- anchors each risk to mitigation and governance structures  

It is the **risk‑to‑governance continuity catalogue** for the envelope.

## **5 — Governance Risk Handling Workflow**

### **Step 1 — Identify risk**  
Triggered by audit, amendment proposal, or contributor review.

### **Step 2 — Map risk to invariant**  
Use the Stability Matrix + Risk Register.

### **Step 3 — Apply mitigation pathway**  
Geometry, semantic, trauma‑informed, or accessibility safeguard.

### **Step 4 — Verify continuity anchor**  
Ensure alignment with Charter + Spec + Ledger.

### **Step 5 — Log governance action**  
Record in Change Log + Change Ledger.

### **Step 6 — Validate stability**  
Run partial or full audit cycle.

This workflow ensures **no risk ever weakens an invariant**.
