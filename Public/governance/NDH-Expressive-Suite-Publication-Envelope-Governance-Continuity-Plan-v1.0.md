# **📜 NDH Expressive Suite Publication Envelope Governance Continuity Plan v1.0**  
### *NDH‑Platforms → Public → governance*

The **Governance Continuity Plan** defines how the NDH Expressive Suite Publication Envelope maintains structural, semantic, and trauma‑informed continuity across all future versions.  
It ensures that the envelope remains stable, reversible, non‑hierarchical, and accessible even as NDH evolves.

This plan is the **long‑term continuity framework** for the Publication Envelope.

---

## **1 — Purpose of the Continuity Plan**

The plan exists to:

- preserve envelope invariants across version transitions  
- ensure trauma‑informed stability over time  
- prevent architectural drift or accidental hierarchy  
- maintain accessibility and metadata integrity  
- define how amendments propagate safely  
- guarantee reversible, non‑hierarchical evolution  

It is the **future‑proofing mechanism** for the Publication Envelope.

---

## **2 — Continuity Principles (Invariant)**

These principles guide all future versions:

- **Reversibility** — no version implies “forward progress” or “finality”  
- **Neutrality** — no version is visually or semantically dominant  
- **Non‑hierarchy** — no version introduces ranking or evaluative language  
- **Trauma‑informed stability** — all geometry remains gentle and reversible  
- **Accessibility continuity** — alt text, tagging, and metadata persist  
- **Governance transparency** — all changes logged in the Change Ledger  

These principles cannot be removed or weakened.

---

## **3 — Version Transition Model**

### **3.1 — Version Format**
```
vMAJOR.MINOR
```

### **3.2 — Major Version Transitions (MAJOR++)**
A major version increment (e.g., v1.x → v2.0) may occur only when:

- new expressive assets are added  
- envelope geometry requires expansion  
- metadata schema evolves  
- accessibility standards change  

Major transitions must:

- preserve all trauma‑informed invariants  
- maintain reversible geometry  
- maintain non‑hierarchical semantics  
- update the Governance Change Ledger  
- trigger a full audit cycle  

### **3.3 — Minor Version Transitions (MINOR++)**
Minor increments (e.g., v1.0 → v1.1) may occur for:

- documentation refinements  
- metadata clarifications  
- accessibility improvements  
- non‑structural adjustments  

Minor transitions must:

- preserve all invariants  
- maintain envelope structure  
- update the Change Log  
- require a partial audit  

---

## **4 — Amendment Propagation Rules**

### **4.1 — Amendments Must Strengthen Invariants**
Any amendment must:

- reinforce trauma‑informed geometry  
- improve accessibility  
- increase clarity  
- maintain neutrality  
- preserve reversibility  

### **4.2 — Amendments Must Not Introduce**
- hierarchy  
- urgency  
- evaluative symbolism  
- directional cues  
- saturated colors  
- asymmetry  

### **4.3 — Amendments Must Be Logged**
Every amendment must be recorded in:

- the **Governance Change Log**  
- the **Governance Change Ledger**  
- the **Audit Ledger** (if structural)  

---

## **5 — Stewardship Continuity**

### **5.1 — Stewardship Body**
Envelope governance is maintained by:

```
NDH-Platforms — Expressive Layer Governance Group (ELGG)
```

### **5.2 — Stewardship Responsibilities**
ELGG must:

- maintain all envelope documents  
- validate each version  
- run full audit cycles  
- update the Change Ledger  
- preserve trauma‑informed invariants  
- ensure accessibility compliance  
- maintain metadata continuity  

Stewardship is ongoing and non‑hierarchical.

---

## **6 — Continuity Safeguards**

### **6.1 — Drift Prevention**
The envelope must never:

- gain or lose governed surfaces  
- reorder surfaces  
- alter page geometry  
- weaken trauma‑informed invariants  
- reduce accessibility metadata  

### **6.2 — Stability Anchors**
The envelope is anchored by:

- the Governance Charter  
- the Envelope Spec  
- the Audit Ledger  
- the Change Ledger  
- the Export Checklist  

These documents form the envelope’s **governance spine**.

---

## **7 — Future‑Version Continuity Workflow**

### **Step 1 — Proposal**
Contributor submits amendment proposal.

### **Step 2 — Review**
ELGG evaluates trauma‑informed, accessibility, and geometry impact.

### **Step 3 — Approval**
Amendment approved only if invariants are strengthened.

### **Step 4 — Logging**
Amendment logged in Change Log + Change Ledger.

### **Step 5 — Version Increment**
Minor or major version increment applied.

### **Step 6 — Audit**
Full or partial audit performed.

### **Step 7 — Publication**
New version released within governed envelope.
