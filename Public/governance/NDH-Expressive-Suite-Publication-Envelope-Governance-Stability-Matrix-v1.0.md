# **📊 NDH Expressive Suite Publication Envelope Governance Stability Matrix v1.0**  
### *NDH‑Platforms → Public → governance*

The **Governance Stability Matrix** maps each constitutional invariant of the Publication Envelope to its associated risks, safeguards, and continuity anchors.  
It ensures that the envelope remains stable, reversible, trauma‑informed, and non‑hierarchical across all future versions.

This matrix is the **governance stability engine** of the expressive‑layer publication system.

---

## **1 — Purpose of the Stability Matrix**

The matrix:

- identifies stability‑critical invariants  
- maps risks that could weaken or distort them  
- defines safeguards that prevent drift  
- anchors each invariant to long‑term governance structures  
- ensures continuity across version transitions  
- supports ELGG stewardship and audit cycles  

It is the **risk‑to‑invariant governance map** for the Publication Envelope.

## **3 — Matrix Interpretation Guide**

### **3.1 — Invariant Column**
Each invariant is a constitutional rule that must remain stable across all versions.

### **3.2 — Risk Column**
Risks are *not failures* — they are potential drift vectors that the governance system must guard against.

### **3.3 — Safeguard Column**
Safeguards are active mechanisms that prevent drift, including:

- trauma‑informed constraints  
- geometry rules  
- semantic filters  
- accessibility requirements  
- audit cycles  

### **3.4 — Continuity Anchor Column**
Anchors are the governance documents that guarantee long‑term stability:

- Governance Charter  
- Envelope Spec  
- Audit Log  
- Audit Ledger  
- Change Log  
- Continuity Plan  

These anchors ensure invariants persist across v1.x → v2.x → v3.x.

## **5 — Stability Enforcement Workflow**

### **Step 1 — Identify invariant at risk**  
Triggered by audit or amendment proposal.

### **Step 2 — Consult safeguard**  
Apply trauma‑informed, semantic, or geometric constraints.

### **Step 3 — Verify continuity anchor**  
Ensure the invariant is still aligned with Charter + Spec.

### **Step 4 — Log governance action**  
Record in Change Log + Change Ledger.

### **Step 5 — Validate stability**  
Run partial or full audit cycle.

This workflow ensures **no invariant ever weakens**.
