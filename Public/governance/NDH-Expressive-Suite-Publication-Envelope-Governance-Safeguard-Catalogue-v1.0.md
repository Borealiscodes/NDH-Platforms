Safeguards operate continuously — preventing drift, blocking hierarchy, and ensuring stability across all future versions.

This catalogue is the **preventative governance layer** of the expressive‑suite envelope.

The catalogue:

It is the **governance shield** of the Publication Envelope.

## **3 — Safeguard Profiles (Full Catalogue)**

### **3.1 — Geometry Reversibility Enforcement**  
Protects: **Reversible Geometry**  
Mechanism:  
- Blocks arrows, chevrons, directional icons  
- Prevents gradients implying flow  
- Enforces centered, reversible geometry  
Anchor: Governance Charter §3.2  

---

### **3.2 — Semantic Neutrality Filter**  
Protects: **Non‑Hierarchical Semantics**  
Mechanism:  
- Removes ranking terms (“primary”, “core”, “final”)  
- Enforces neutral, reversible phrasing  
Anchor: Trauma‑Informed Invariants Block  

---

### **3.3 — Centering & Margin Guard**  
Protects: **Centered Alignment**  
Mechanism:  
- Enforces 40–60 px margins  
- Ensures all elements remain centered  
Anchor: Envelope Geometry Specification  

---

### **3.4 — Pastel Palette Constraint**  
Protects: **Pastel Palette**  
Mechanism:  
- Blocks saturated or urgency colors  
- Enforces pastel palette  
Anchor: Trauma‑Informed Design Rules  

---

### **3.5 — Accessibility Metadata Enforcement**  
Protects: **Accessibility Metadata**  
Mechanism:  
- Requires alt text for all figures  
- Enforces `<Figure>`, `<Section>`, `<Figcaption>` tagging  
Anchor: Accessibility Requirements Spec  

---

### **3.6 — Canonical Surface Order Lock**  
Protects: **Fixed Surface Order**  
Mechanism:  
- Locks page order  
- Prevents reordering or insertion of new surfaces  
Anchor: Envelope Spec §3.1  

---

### **3.7 — Equal Visual Weight Normalizer**  
Protects: **Equal Visual Weight**  
Mechanism:  
- Normalizes PNG sizes (350–450 px)  
- Enforces consistent grid spacing  
Anchor: Export Checklist §2  

---

### **3.8 — Typography Neutrality Constraint**  
Protects: **Neutral Typography**  
Mechanism:  
- Blocks bold, italics, uppercase emphasis  
- Enforces soft gray (#6A6A6A)  
Anchor: Envelope Spec §4  

---

### **3.9 — Metadata Continuity Guard**  
Protects: **Metadata Continuity**  
Mechanism:  
- Enforces canonical metadata fields  
- Prevents drift in title, keywords, version  
Anchor: Metadata Block v1.0  

---

### **3.10 — Audit Traceability Enforcement**  
Protects: **Audit Traceability**  
Mechanism:  
- Requires Audit Log + Audit Ledger updates  
- Ensures reversible historical continuity  
Anchor: Governance Change Ledger

## **5 — Safeguard Activation Workflow**

### **Step 1 — Detect potential drift**  
Triggered by audit, contributor review, or amendment proposal.

### **Step 2 — Identify threatened invariant**  
Use the Stability Matrix + Risk Register.

### **Step 3 — Activate corresponding safeguard**  
Apply geometry, semantic, accessibility, or metadata protection.

### **Step 4 — Validate continuity anchor alignment**  
Ensure safeguard aligns with Charter + Spec + Ledger.

### **Step 5 — Log governance action**  
Record in Change Log + Change Ledger.

### **Step 6 — Run audit cycle**  
Partial or full audit depending on severity.

Safeguards ensure **drift is prevented before it becomes a risk**.
