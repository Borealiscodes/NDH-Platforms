# **📄 NDH Expressive Suite Consolidated PDF Back Page v1.0**  
### *NDH‑Platforms → Public → README*

The **Back Page** is the final surface of the NDH Expressive Suite Consolidated PDF.  
It provides a calm, reflective closing that reinforces NDH trauma‑informed principles and the expressive suite’s role within the broader NDH ecosystem.

---

## **1 — Purpose of the Back Page**

The back page:

- closes the expressive suite with a gentle, non‑hierarchical surface  
- reinforces NDH trauma‑informed invariants  
- provides a soft conceptual fade‑out rather than a hard ending  
- maintains emotional neutrality and reversibility  
- anchors the expressive suite within NDH governance  

It is the **official closing matter** for the expressive suite.

---

## **2 — Required Elements**

### **2.1 — Closing Statement**

Centered, soft‑gray text:

```
The NDH Expressive Suite is a governed, trauma-informed visual ecosystem.
All surfaces are reversible, non-hierarchical, and emotionally neutral.
```

### **2.2 — Soft Fade Geometry**

A gentle lavender fade at the bottom of the page:

- 10–15% opacity  
- No hard edges  
- No gradients implying direction  
- No framing boxes  

### **2.3 — NDH Expressive Ecosystem Line**

Centered, soft‑gray text:

```
Traversal • Public • Activation
A unified constellation of governed expressive assets
```

### **2.4 — Optional Minimal Iconography**

A **single soft circle** (20–30 px) centered near the bottom:

- pastel lavender (#EDE7FF)  
- 20% opacity  
- no label  
- no symbolism  
- no hierarchy  

This circle represents **reversibility**, not a “final node.”

---

## **3 — Typography Rules**

- Font: Rounded sans‑serif  
- Closing statement: 24–28 px  
- Ecosystem line: 20–24 px  
- Color: Soft gray (#6A6A6A)  
- No bold  
- No italics  
- No uppercase emphasis  

---

## **4 — Layout Geometry**

### **Page Size**
- A4 or US Letter  
- Portrait orientation  
- 40–60 px margins  

### **Vertical Flow**
1. Closing statement  
2. Ecosystem line  
3. Optional soft circle  
4. Lavender fade  

### **Alignment**
- All elements centered  
- No left‑aligned or right‑aligned blocks  
- No asymmetry implying hierarchy  

---

## **5 — Trauma‑Informed Constraints**

- No urgency colors  
- No directional arrows  
- No evaluative language  
- No “end”, “final”, “complete”, “primary”, “secondary” terminology  
- No saturated colors  
- No sharp geometry  
- No logos or branding marks  
- No “closing remarks” tone — only gentle fade  

The back page must feel **calm, reversible, and emotionally neutral**.
