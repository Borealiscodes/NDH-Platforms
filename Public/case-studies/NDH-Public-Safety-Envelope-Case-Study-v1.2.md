# ⭐ 1 — What Is the NDH Safety Envelope?

The NDH Safety Envelope is the **soft boundary** that keeps exploration inside NDH safe.

It ensures:

- no overwhelming paths  
- no confusing jumps  
- no sudden changes  
- no emotional spikes  
- no getting stuck  
- no being forced into anything  

Think of it like a **calm, glowing outline** around the NDH world.

# ⭐ 2 — Why NDH Needs a Safety Envelope

NDH is a world full of:

- ideas  
- connections

- paths  

Some paths go deeper than others.  
Some ideas connect in surprising ways.

# ⭐ 3 — How NDH Keeps Exploration Predictable

NDH uses three public‑facing safety principles:

### **1 — Clear Paths**  
You always see where you’re going.

### **2 — Gentle Guidance**  
Friendly guardians (Sneks) help you stay oriented.

### **3 — Reversible Steps**  
You can always step back.
