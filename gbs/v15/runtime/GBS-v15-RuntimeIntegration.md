## **III. Runtime Signal Flow in GBS v15**

### **1. Observation (BR‑RT)**  
GBS v15 receives raw behavioral signals from BR‑RT:

- tendencies  
- anomalies  
- drift patterns  
- expressive ontology behavior  
- dimensional noise  

BR‑RT does **not** correct behavior — it only observes and signals.

### **2. Intersectional Filtering (IR‑Spec)**  
IR‑Spec filters BR‑RT signals for:

- bias  
- cultural drift  
- exclusionary patterns  
- trauma‑sensitive violations  
- dominance metaphors  

Filtered signals are passed upward to OG‑RT.

### **3. Governance Correction (OG‑RT)**  
OG‑RT applies:

- trajectory correction  
- orbital stabilization  
- dimensional alignment  
- governance field balancing  

OG‑RT does **not** define governance physics — it executes them.

### **4. Governance Physics (OG‑Spec)**  
OG‑Spec defines:

- invariant core  
- orbital layers  
- perimeter boundaries  
- correction physics  

GBS v15 must remain aligned with OG‑Spec at all times.
