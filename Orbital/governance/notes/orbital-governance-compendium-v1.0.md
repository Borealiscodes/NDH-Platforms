## **1. Purpose**

The Governance Compendium is the **multi‑volume library** that:

- merges all governance reference artifacts  
- organizes them into a coherent library structure  
- provides a single entry point for all governance knowledge  
- ensures maintainers can navigate the entire governance ecosystem  
- prevents fragmentation of governance knowledge  
- preserves naming, provenance, rhythm, and structural invariants across time  

It is the “governance archive” of NDH.

---

## **2. Compendium Structure (Multi‑Volume)**

The Compendium consists of **six volumes**, each representing a major governance domain.

### **Volume I — Foundations**
Contains:
- **Governance Constitution**  
- **Governance Enforcement Charter**  

Purpose: define *why* governance exists and *what* it may enforce.

---

### **Volume II — Naming & Semantics**
Contains:
- **Naming Invariant Ledger**  
- **Governance Lexicon**

### **Volume III — Structure & Style**

- **Governance Style Guide**  
- **Governance Banner**

### **Volume IV — Enforcement Machinery**

The Compendium uses a **three‑layer navigation model**:

### **Layer 1 — Volumes**
