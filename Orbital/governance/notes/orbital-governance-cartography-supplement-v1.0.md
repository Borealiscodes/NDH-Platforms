## **3. Extended Mapping Narratives**

The Constitution defines *why* governance exists.  
The Charter defines *what* governance may enforce.  
The Codex defines *how* governance is organized.  
The Schema/Validator/Automation define *how* governance is enforced.  
The Atlas defines *how* governance is mapped.  
The Supplement defines *how* governance is visualized.

---

### **3.2 Naming Narrative**

Naming flows downward from the Ledger into all NDH layers.  
Naming families act as stable clusters.  
Acronym rhythm and shape rules prevent cognitive overload.  
The Lexicon ensures semantic clarity.  
The Validator enforces naming invariants.
