## **1. Purpose**

The Enforcement Charter establishes Orbital’s:

- governance authority  
- enforcement jurisdiction  
- naming‑invariant power  
- documentation‑rhythm power  
- provenance‑boundary power  
- accessibility‑safeguard power  
- cross‑repo oversight  

It is the formal declaration that Orbital governs NDH naming and documentation across all layers.

---

## **2. Governance Authority**

Orbital is granted full authority over:

- **naming invariants**  
- **documentation rhythm**  
- **provenance boundaries**  
- **cross‑repo linkage rules**  
- **structural governance schema**  
- **validator enforcement**  
- **automation enforcement**  

This authority is binding across NDH‑CORE, NDH‑TIDS, and Emergent.

---

## **3. Enforcement Jurisdiction**

Orbital governs:

### **NDH‑CORE**  
Operator naming, runtime boundary naming, operator ergonomics.

### **NDH‑TIDS**  
Topology naming, holonomy naming, manifold boundary naming.

### **Emergent**  
Interpretive naming, cognitive ergonomics, trauma‑informed naming.

### **Orbital**  
Governance naming, invariant naming, documentation naming.

Orbital’s jurisdiction is cross‑layer and cross‑repo.

---

## **4. Enforcement Powers**

Orbital is empowered to:

- enforce naming invariants  
- enforce acronym rhythm  
- enforce shape‑consistency  
- enforce provenance anchors  
- enforce lineage markers  
- enforce cross‑repo linkage  
- enforce accessibility rules  
- enforce schema compliance  
- enforce validator compliance  
- enforce automation compliance  

These powers ensure NDH naming remains stable and safe.

---

## **5. Enforcement Mechanisms**

Orbital enforces governance through:

- **Governance Schema**  
- **Governance Validator**  
- **Governance Automation Spec**  
- **Naming Invariant Ledger**  
- **Governance Style Guide**  
- **Governance Lexicon**  

These artifacts form the enforcement framework.

---

## **6. Enforcement Boundaries**

Orbital must:

- preserve cross‑layer separation  
- prevent naming contamination  
- prevent rhythm drift  
- prevent shape drift  
- prevent provenance violations  
- prevent accessibility violations  
- prevent documentation drift  

These boundaries protect NDH’s architectural integrity.

---

## **7. Enforcement Obligations**

Orbital is obligated to:

- maintain naming stability  
- maintain documentation rhythm  
- maintain provenance clarity  
- maintain accessibility safety  
- maintain cross‑repo consistency  
- maintain architectural coherence  

These obligations ensure NDH remains stable and inclusive.

---

## **8. Enforcement Limitations**

Orbital must not:

- override repo‑specific functional naming  
- introduce naming families outside the Ledger  
- modify NDH‑CORE operator semantics  
- modify NDH‑TIDS topological semantics  
- modify Emergent interpretive semantics  

Orbital governs naming and documentation, not runtime behavior.

---

The Charter must reference:

These documents define the suite’s governance structure.
