## **1. Purpose**

The Orbital Governance Lexicon defines:

- governance terminology  
- naming‑family definitions  
- provenance vocabulary  
- cross‑layer architectural terms  
- rhythm and shape descriptors  
- dyslexia‑aware naming concepts  
- NDH‑specific semantic invariants  

Orbital maintains this lexicon to ensure semantic stability across all governance documents.

---

## **2. Core Governance Terms**

- **Orbital Governance Note** — A document authored at the Orbital layer defining cross‑repo rules, invariants, or boundaries.  
- **Provenance Boundary** — A rule defining where naming or architectural lineage must not cross.  
- **Naming Invariant** — A stable naming rule that must remain unchanged across NDH layers.  
- **Cross‑Layer Alignment** — The requirement that NDH‑CORE, NDH‑TIDS, Orbital, and Emergent follow consistent naming and documentation rules.  
- **Documentation Rhythm** — The predictable cadence and structural flow required in governance documents.  
- **Shape‑Consistency** — The visual and phonological stability of acronyms and naming families.

## **4. Rhythm & Shape Terms**

- **Acronym Rhythm** — The cadence and phonological pattern of NDH acronyms.  
- **Shape‑Family** — A cluster of acronyms sharing visual and phonological form.  
- **Rhythm Drift** — A violation of expected naming cadence.  
- **Shape Drift** — A violation of expected acronym form.  
- **Pattern‑Collapse** — Dyslexic cognitive overload caused by naming inconsistency.

---

## **5. Provenance Terms**

- **Lineage Marker** — A naming element indicating architectural ancestry.  
- **Boundary Enforcer** — A construct ensuring naming or architectural separation.  
- **Provenance Anchor** — A stable reference point used to maintain naming lineage.  
- **Cross‑Repo Provenance** — The requirement that naming lineage be preserved across NDH repos.

---

## **6. Accessibility & Dyslexia‑Aware Terms**

- **Interpretive Safety** — The requirement that naming and documentation avoid cognitive stress triggers.  
- **Stress‑Load Spike** — A sudden increase in cognitive load caused by naming irregularities.  
- **Narrative Stability** — The consistency of interpretive scaffolding across documents.  
- **Cognitive Rhythm** — The predictable flow of naming and documentation required for dyslexic accessibility.

---

## **7. Cross‑Repo Terms**

- **Suite Artifact** — Any document belonging to the NDH Naming Case Study Suite.  
- **Cross‑Repo Linkage** — The requirement that documents reference the Meta‑Document, Blueprint, and Index.  
- **Layer Boundary** — The architectural separation between NDH‑CORE, NDH‑TIDS, Orbital, and Emergent.
