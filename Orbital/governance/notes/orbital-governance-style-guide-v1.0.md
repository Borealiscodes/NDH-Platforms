## **1. Purpose**

The Orbital Governance Style Guide defines:

- structural rules  
- naming rules  
- rhythm rules  
- provenance markers  
- cross‑repo linkage requirements  
- document framing conventions  
- accessibility and dyslexia‑aware writing rules  

Orbital maintains this guide to ensure governance documents remain stable, predictable, and safe for dyslexic developers.

---

## **2. Required Document Structure**

All Orbital governance documents must include:

### **2.1 Governance Banner (Top)**  
The **Orbital Governance Banner** must appear at the top of every governance document.

### **2.2 Section Ordering**  
Documents must follow this order:

1. Purpose  
2. Context  
3. Architectural Impact  
4. Requirements  
5. Recommendations  
6. Cross‑Repo Linkage  
7. Provenance  
8. Commit Description (if applicable)

### **2.3 Governance Footer (Bottom)**  
The **Orbital Governance Footer** must appear at the bottom.

---

## **3. Naming Rules**

Orbital enforces strict naming invariants:

### **3.1 Use Canonical Naming Families**  
All naming must follow the families defined in the **Naming Invariant Ledger**.

### **3.2 Maintain Acronym Shape‑Consistency**  
Acronyms must follow:

- 3–4 letter clusters  
- stable consonant‑vowel rhythm  
- predictable visual shape  

### **3.3 Avoid Naming Outliers**  
No sudden 6‑letter acronyms.  
No inconsistent rhythm.  
No cross‑layer contamination.

---

## **4. Rhythm Rules**

Orbital documents must maintain:

- consistent syllable cadence  
- predictable stress patterns  
- stable acronym phonology  
- rhythm alignment across sections  

Rhythm instability increases dyslexic cognitive load.

---

## **5. Provenance Markers**

Every Orbital governance document must include:

- explicit provenance scope  
- cross‑layer alignment markers  
- suite membership (if applicable)  
- references to the Meta‑Document  

Provenance markers prevent architectural drift.

---

## **6. Cross‑Repo Linkage Requirements**

Orbital documents must link to:

- **Meta‑Document**  
- **Refinement Blueprint**  
- **Suite Index**  

These links ensure cross‑layer consistency.

---

## **7. Accessibility & Dyslexia‑Aware Rules**

Orbital documents must:

- avoid dense acronym clusters  
- maintain consistent rhythm  
- use predictable section ordering  
- avoid visually similar acronyms with different meanings  
- avoid stress‑triggering naming patterns (see Ledger exclusions)

These rules ensure governance documents remain safe for dyslexic developers.

---

## **8. Formatting Rules**

- Use H2/H3 headers only  
- Avoid nested lists  
- Keep paragraphs short  
- Maintain consistent indentation  
- Use bold sparingly  
- Avoid italics for acronyms  
- Use guided links for cross‑repo references  

---

## **9. Placement Rules**

All Orbital governance documents must be placed under:

```
NDH-Platforms/Orbital/governance/notes/
```

This ensures structural consistency.
