## **1. Purpose**

The Governance Encyclopedia provides:

- an A–Z reference of all governance terminology  
- definitions of naming invariants  
- explanations of provenance concepts  
- descriptions of structural rules  
- summaries of enforcement machinery  
- cross‑repo linkage references  
- diagram references  
- temporal governance concepts  
- accessibility and dyslexia‑aware terminology  

It is the **complete governance knowledge base**.

---

## **2. Encyclopedia Structure**

The Encyclopedia is organized alphabetically:

- **A–F**: Authority, Accessibility, Artifacts  
- **G–L**: Governance, Lexicon, Ledger  
- **M–R**: Meta‑Document, Rhythm, Provenance  
- **S–Z**: Schema, Style, Validator, Workflow  

Each entry includes:

- definition  
- governance layer  
- cross‑repo relevance  
- naming‑family relationship  
- provenance impact  
- accessibility notes  
- guided links for deeper exploration
