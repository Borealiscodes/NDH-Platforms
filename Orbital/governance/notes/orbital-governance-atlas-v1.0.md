## **1. Purpose**

The Governance Atlas provides a **structural map** of:

- governance artifacts  
- naming‑invariant artifacts  
- enforcement artifacts  
- temporal artifacts  
- suite infrastructure artifacts  
- cross‑repo relationships  
- provenance flows  
- architectural boundaries  

It is the cartographic overview of the entire NDH governance system.

---

## **2. Atlas Structure**

The Atlas is organized into **five maps**, each representing a different dimension of governance:

1. **Governance Authority Map**  
2. **Naming & Semantic Map**  
3. **Structural & Stylistic Map**  
4. **Enforcement Machinery Map**  
5. **Temporal Governance Map**

Each map shows how artifacts relate to each other.

---

## **3. Governance Authority Map**

This map shows the hierarchy of governance authority:

- **Governance Constitution**  
  ↓  
- **Enforcement Charter**  
  ↓  
- **Orbital Governance Documents**  
  ↓  
- NDH‑CORE / NDH‑TIDS / Emergent (governed layers)

This map establishes the flow of authority.

## **4. Naming & Semantic Map**
