# **NDH‑Orbital Governance Directive**  
## **Global Hyperatlas Construction Pause (v1.0)**  
### *Formal Cross‑Repository Governance Instrument*  
### Indexed as **NDH‑Orbital‑Pause‑001**

---

## **1. Directive Authority**

This directive is issued under **NDH‑Orbital Governance**, the governing body responsible for:

- global routing rules,  
- Sentinel invariant definition,  
- Trauma‑Informed‑Systems‑Design evaluator semantics,  
- GQL virtual machine governance,  
- cross‑repository architectural stability.

This directive supersedes all domain‑level construction permissions across the NDH ecosystem.

---

## **2. Directive Purpose**

To establish a **global, cross‑repository pause** on all Hyperatlas construction activities, ensuring architectural safety while governance, evaluator, and VM integration issues are resolved.

This pause supports refinement of:

- **Orbital Core**  
- **TISD Evaluator Semantics**  
- **Sentinel Invariants**  
- **GQL VM Integration**  
- **TTTTTTP Audit**  

---

## **3. Clarification: GQL VM and Sentinel Are Complete**

This pause is **not** due to deficiencies in:

- the **GQL Virtual Machine**,  
- the **Sentinel Enforcement Engine**,  
- or their underlying bytecode and invariant logic.

Both systems are **complete and operational**.

The pause is required because:

- Copilot **cannot audit NDH repositories**,  
- VS Code hooks for NDH governance are **not yet refined**,  
- Sentinel/GQL integration cannot be validated automatically,  
- manual audit of all repos is **laborious and error‑prone**,  
- EDAD ticket is open to address upload and inspection constraints.

Thus, the pause is due to **tooling limitations**, not architectural incompleteness.

This distinction is formally recorded to prevent misinterpretation by future governance reviews.
