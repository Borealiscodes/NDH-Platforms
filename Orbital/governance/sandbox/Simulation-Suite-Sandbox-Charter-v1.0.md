# **📘 NDH‑Orbital Governance Charter**  
## **Simulation Suite Sandbox Charter (v1.0)**  
### *Formal Governance Instrument*  
### Indexed as **NDH‑Orbital‑Sandbox‑Charter‑001**

---

## **1. Charter Authority**

This charter is issued under **NDH‑Orbital Governance**, the governing body responsible for:

- global NDH routing rules,  
- Sentinel invariant definition,  
- Trauma‑Informed‑Systems‑Design evaluator semantics,  
- GQL Virtual Machine governance,  
- cross‑repository architectural stability.

This charter establishes the **Simulation Suite Sandbox** as the exclusive environment for architectural validation during the global Hyperatlas construction pause.

---

## **2. Charter Purpose**

To formally reopen and authorize the **NDH‑Simulation‑Suite** as a **sandbox environment** for:

- validating **Orbital Core** v1.0,  
- populating **TTTTTTP Audit** v1.0,  
- testing **GQL Virtual Machine** behavior,  
- validating **TISD Evaluator** PASS/WARN/FAIL semantics,  
- simulating **Sentinel Invariant** enforcement,  
- ensuring cross‑repo routing consistency.

The sandbox is required due to current tooling limitations preventing safe automated validation.

---

## **3. Clarification: GQL VM and Sentinel Are Complete**

This charter formally records:

- The **GQL Virtual Machine** is complete and operational.  
- The **Sentinel Enforcement Engine** is complete and operational.  
- Their bytecode, invariant logic, and evaluator interfaces are stable.

The need for sandbox validation arises from:

- unrefined VS Code governance hooks,  
- Copilot’s inability to audit NDH repositories,  
- laborious manual audit requirements,  
- active EDAD ticket addressing upload/inspection constraints.

This distinction prevents misinterpretation of the pause as architectural incompleteness.

---

## **4. Sandbox Mandate**

The Simulation Suite Sandbox is authorized to:

### **4.1 Validate Orbital Core v1.0**
- governance routing rules,  
- invariant centralization,  
- evaluator boundaries,  
- VM interaction semantics.

### **4.2 Populate TTTTTTP Audit v1.0**
- using sandbox‑validated governance data,  
- ensuring audit criteria reflect actual system behavior.

### **4.3 Test GQL VM Behavior**
- manifold execution,  
- bytecode traversal,  
- evaluator invocation,  
- Sentinel enforcement triggers.

### **4.4 Validate TISD Evaluator Semantics**
- PASS/WARN/FAIL correctness,  
- trauma‑informed constraint enforcement,  
- evaluator boundaries.

### **4.5 Simulate Sentinel Enforcement**
- invariant enforcement,  
- quarantine logic,  
- escalation pathways.

### **4.6 Ensure Cross‑Repo Routing Consistency**
- NDH‑PLATFORMS,  
- NDH‑CORE,  
- Trauma‑Informed‑Systems‑Design,  
- NDH‑TIDS,  
- NDH‑Serenity‑Manifold.

---

## **5. Sandbox Boundaries**

### **5.1 Sandbox Is Non‑Production**
No changes made in the sandbox propagate automatically to NDH‑TIDS or NDH‑PLATFORMS.

### **5.2 Sandbox Is Exclusive**
All architectural validation must occur in the sandbox until the pause is lifted.

### **5.3 Sandbox Is Authoritative for Testing**
Only sandbox results may be used to populate:

- Orbital Core v1.0,  
- TTTTTTP Audit v1.0,  
- Sentinel invariant centralization,  
- evaluator semantics.

### **5.4 Sandbox Does Not Permit Hyperatlas Construction**
Hyperatlas construction remains paused globally.

---

## **6. Sandbox Success Criteria**

The sandbox must demonstrate:

1. Stable GQL VM execution across representative manifolds.  
2. Correct TISD evaluator PASS/WARN/FAIL behavior.  
3. Reliable Sentinel invariant enforcement.  
4. No routing drift across NDH repositories.  
5. Complete TTTTTTP Audit v1.0 criteria.  
6. Fully validated Orbital Core v1.0.

Only after these criteria are met may the global pause be lifted.

---

## **7. Charter Duration**

This charter remains in effect until:

- TTTTTTP Audit v1.0 is complete,  
- Orbital Core v1.0 is validated,  
- Sentinel invariants are centralized,  
- TISD evaluator semantics are finalized,  
- GQL VM integration is confirmed stable.
