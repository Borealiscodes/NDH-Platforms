# ⭐ **1. Purpose**

The Holonomy Safety Envelope Specification defines:

- how safety envelopes behave under holonomy transformation  
- how Omega‑V stabilizes envelope geometry  
- how Ω4 enforces envelope invariants  
- how GBS v13 orchestrates envelope propagation  
- how envelopes behave under rollback (GBS v12)  
- how envelopes behave under forward holonomy (post‑v13)  
- how ERP uses envelope surfaces during emergency response  
- how envelopes integrate with the Index → Meta Index → Meta‑Meta Index pipeline  

This is the **safety geometry** of NDH governance.

---

# 🧭 **2. Safety Envelope Geometry Context**

Safety envelopes operate on the **Pentachoron Governance Manifold**, composed of:

- Registry Vertex  
- Ledger Vertex  
- Sentinel Vertex  
- Orchestrator Vertex  
- Omega‑V Vertex  

Safety envelopes define the **governance boundary conditions** across all vertices and surfaces.

GBS v13 orchestrates envelope geometry.

---

# 🧩 **3. Safety Envelope Types**

Holonomy safety envelopes include:

### **1. Naming Safety Envelope**  
Protects naming invariants from contamination and drift.

### **2. Provenance Safety Envelope**  
Protects lineage invariants from invalid traversal and DAG corruption.

### **3. Structural Safety Envelope**  
Protects compendium volumes, sections, and structural invariants.

### **4. Enforcement Safety Envelope**  
Protects enforcement rules from contradiction or unsafe evolution.

### **5. Temporal Safety Envelope**  
Protects year/quarter invariants from contradiction or temporal drift.

Each envelope must remain holonomy‑consistent.

# 🌀 **4. Omega‑V Safety Stabilization**

Omega‑V is the **holonomic anchor** that stabilizes all safety envelopes under:

- version evolution  
- envelope tightening  
- envelope expansion  
- temporal holonomy shifts  
- emergent case study evolution  

Omega‑V must remain:

- lineage‑anchored  
- safety‑validated  
- globally propagated  
- rollback‑compatible  
- forward‑holonomy‑compatible  

Omega‑V is the only envelope anchor allowed to evolve.

# 🔗 **5. Ω4 Enforcement Coupling**

Ω4 enforces safety envelopes across:

- vertices

Ω4 must:

# 🧭 **8. Rollback Safety (GBS v12 → GBS v13)**

Rollback safety is defined as a **holonomy contraction** from:

**Pentachoron → Triad**

Rollback must preserve:

- naming safety  
- provenance safety  
- structural safety  
- enforcement safety  
- temporal safety  

Rollback surfaces:

- `rollback.surface.safety`  
- `rollback.surface.holonomy`  
- `rollback.surface.envelope`  
- `rollback.surface.lineage`  

Ω4 and Omega‑V must remain stable during rollback.
