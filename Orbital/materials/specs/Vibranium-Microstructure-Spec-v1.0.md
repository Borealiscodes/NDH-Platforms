### 4. Energy interaction micro‑mechanisms

- **Absorption:**  
  - Incident kinetic energy excites resonance bonds → energy diverted into harmonic wells  
- **Storage:**  
  - Micro‑wells hold energy as localized vibrational modes  
- **Redistribution:**  
  - Resonance channels route energy away from impact site, flattening stress gradients  
- **Release:**  
  - Controlled: slow bleed via low‑frequency damping  
  - Uncontrolled: harmonic cascade → explosive discharge when saturation threshold exceeded

### 5. Mechanical properties at micro‑scale

- **Local stiffness:** Variable, frequency‑dependent  
- **Yield behavior:**  
  - Micro‑yield occurs as bond‑phase shift, not plastic deformation  
  - Macro‑yield delayed by energy routing and storage  
- **Fatigue:**  
  - Low under sub‑threshold cyclic loading (energy never reaches saturation)  
  - High risk if repeatedly driven near saturation (micro‑wells destabilize)

### 6. Failure modes (microstructural)

- **Resonance overload:**

- **Channel collapse:**  
  - Resonance channels fail to route energy → localized catastrophic fracture  
- **Harmonic fracture:**  
  - Sudden phase inversion → shock front propagates through lattice → brittle‑like failure in overloaded regions
