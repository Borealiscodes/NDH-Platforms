## ⭐ **1. Purpose of the Hybrid Alloy**

The hybrid alloy is designed to combine:

- **Vibranium’s adaptive resonance**  
- **Adamantium’s absolute rigidity**

The result is a material that:

- absorbs energy without saturating  
- resists deformation without brittleness  
- maintains structural integrity under cosmic‑level stress  
- supports NDH‑style transmission (absorb → route → stabilize → reflect)

This alloy is the **middle path** between two extremes.

# ⚛️ **3. Bonding Strategy**

The hybrid alloy uses **three bond classes**:

### **Bond Class I — Resonant‑Rigid Couplers**
- connect Vibranium resonance channels to Adamantium domains  
- allow energy transfer without lattice collapse  
- maintain rigidity under load  

### **Bond Class II — Harmonic Dampers**
- prevent Vibranium saturation  
- bleed excess energy into Adamantium’s rigid domains  

### **Bond Class III — Stability Anchors**
- enforce NDH‑Stability‑Manifold behavior  
- prevent runaway resonance cascades  
- maintain global lattice coherence

# 🔥 **4. Energy Interaction Profile**

### ASCII — Energy Flow

```text
Incoming Force →
   Vibranium absorbs →
      Resonance channels route →
         Adamantium reflects & stabilizes →
            Hybrid lattice dissipates safely
```

### Behavior:
- **Absorption:** Vibranium handles initial shock
