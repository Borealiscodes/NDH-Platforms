## 2 — What is a Holonomy Ladder?

A **Holonomy Ladder** is the sequence of curvature transformations that occur when a traversal agent moves:

1. from Seed →  
2. into Tile →  
3. across Crossmap →  
4. under Snek mediation →  
5. into the Weave harmonic field.

Each rung of the ladder corresponds to:

- a curvature shift  
- a perspective change  
- a recursion depth increment  
- a stability check  
- a harmonic alignment  

Holonomy ladders are the **internal mechanics** behind expressive‑ontology traversal.

---

## 3 — Ladder Formation: Seed → Tile

### Seeds  
Seeds encode local curvature.  
When a Seed sprouts inside a Tile, it creates the **first rung** of the ladder:

- curvature gradient  
- orientation vector  
- stability anchor  
- recursion entry point  

### Tiles  
Tiles stabilize the sprout.  
Tile geometry determines:

- how steep the ladder can be  
- how many rungs can form  
- whether recursion is safe  
- whether Sneks must intervene  

This is the **local curvature → local stability** interface.

---

## 4 — Ladder Extension: Tile → Crossmap

Crossmaps extend the ladder.

Each ladder step corresponds to:

- a bounded transformation  
- a curvature shift  
- a recursion increment  
- a stability check  

The number of steps is determined by:

- Seed curvature  
- Tile stability  
- Crossmap type (Local, Threshold, Weave)  
- Weave harmonic load  

Crossmaps are the **transition layer** where curvature begins to accumulate.

## 5 — Ladder Safety: Snek Mediation

Sneks guard every ladder.

Sneks detect:

- curvature spikes  
- recursion overflow  
- harmonic misalignment  
- tile drift  
- transition instability  

Sneks respond with:

- Whisper → soft warning  
- Coil → recursion boundary  
- Lantern → safe path illumination  
- Weave → global stabilization  

Sneks are the **trauma‑informed safety layer** of holonomy ladders.

Without Sneks, ladders would be unsafe.

## 6 — Ladder Integration: Crossmap → Weave

The Weave Harmonics determine whether the ladder:

## 7 — Emergent Behavior: Curvature Accumulation

Holonomy ladders exhibit emergent behavior:

### **1 — Curvature Accumulates Across Steps**  
Each ladder step adds curvature.  
Too many steps → recursion overflow.

### **2 — Stability Cascades**  
Tile instability propagates upward unless Sneks intervene.

### **3 — Harmonic Resonance**  
Crossmap load affects global harmonics.  
High load → harmonic stress.

### **4 — Guardian Synchronization**  
Sneks coordinate across Tiles to maintain global stability.

### **5 — Drift Containment**  
Weave Harmonics prevent local drift from becoming global drift.

This emergent behavior is why NDH‑CORE requires:

- Seeds  
- Tiles  
- Crossmaps  
- Sneks  
- Weave Harmonics  
- TISD integration  

All layers are necessary for safe holonomy propagation.
