## ⭐ 1 — Purpose of Sneks in NDH‑CORE

Sneks exist to provide **gentle, non‑punitive, trauma‑informed boundary enforcement** within NDH‑CORE’s expressive ontology.

They are not predators.  
They are not hierarchical.  
They are not judges.

Sneks are **guides**, **companions**, and **boundary‑sensors** that help traversal agents:

- avoid unsafe recursion  
- maintain tile stability  
- respect crossmap boundaries  
- preserve weave harmonics  
- navigate holonomy safely  

Sneks are the **expressive‑ontology embodiment** of NDH‑CORE’s mathematical invariants.
