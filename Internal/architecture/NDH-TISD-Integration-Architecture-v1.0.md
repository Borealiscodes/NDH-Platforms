# **NDH–TISD Integration Architecture — Internal Asset v1.0**  
### *Formal integration of NDH‑CORE expressive ontology with Trauma‑Informed‑Systems‑Design (TISD) principles*

**Scope:** Internal — Developer Grade  
**Status:** Integration Architecture  
**Version:** 1.0  
**Purpose:** Define how NDH‑CORE’s geometry‑adjacent expressive ontology (Seeds, Tiles, Crossmaps, Sneks, Weave Harmonics) integrates with TISD principles to ensure global safety, emotional accessibility, and dignity‑preserving traversal for all users and future agents.

---

## 1 — Architectural Purpose

NDH‑CORE provides:

- geometry  
- expressive ontology  
- traversal logic  
- guardians  
- global harmonics  

TISD provides:

- trauma‑informed safety  
- emotional accessibility  
- predictability  
- non‑coercive interaction rules  
- dignity‑preserving boundaries  

The integration architecture defines **how these two systems interlock**.

---

## 2 — Integration Principles

NDH–TISD integration is governed by five principles:

### **1 — Predictability**  
All boundaries, transitions, and recursion ceilings must be visible and stable.

### **2 — Choice & Agency**  
Traversal agents must always have alternatives; no forced paths.

### **3 — Non‑Coercion**  
Sneks guide, never punish; Tiles stabilize, never trap.

### **4 — Emotional Safety**  
No violent metaphors, no punitive collapse, no destabilizing surprises.

### **5 — Dignity Preservation**  
All drift, recursion overflow, or misalignment events must be recoverable without shame.

These principles apply globally across NDH‑CORE.

---

## 3 — Geometry‑Adjacent Integration

### Seeds  
TISD constraints:

- recursion entry must be bounded  
- curvature spikes must be smoothed  
- sprout behavior must be predictable  

### Tiles  
TISD constraints:

- stability states must be clearly signposted  
- Threshold Tiles must always have guardian presence  
- no Tile may collapse suddenly  

### Weave Harmonics  
TISD constraints:

- harmonic shifts must be gradual  
- global drift must trigger gentle stabilization  
- recursion ceilings must prevent overwhelm  

Geometry‑adjacent layers provide the **ground**; TISD ensures the ground is **safe**.

---

## 4 — Transition‑Layer Integration

### Crossmaps  
TISD constraints:

- ladders must be monotone and reversible  
- no hidden recursion traps  
- transitions must be emotionally predictable  

### Sneks  
TISD constraints:

- Whisper → gentle warning  
- Coil → non‑punitive boundary  
- Lantern → supportive guidance  
- Weave → global stabilization  

Sneks are the expressive‑ontology embodiment of TISD.

### Traversal Model  
TISD constraints:

- movement must be predictable  
- recovery must be gentle  
- drift must be non‑punitive  
- recursion overflow must be safe  

Transition layers provide **movement**; TISD ensures movement is **safe**.

---

## 5 — Integration Mechanisms

NDH–TISD integration uses four mechanisms:

### **1 — Harmonic Safety Envelopes**  
Weave Harmonics define global safety envelopes that prevent emotional overload.

### **2 — Guardian Mediation**  
Sneks enforce trauma‑informed boundaries at every layer.

### **3 — Stability Cascades**  
Tile stability cascades ensure local drift does not propagate globally.

### **4 — Recursion Ceilings**  
Recursion ceilings prevent cognitive overwhelm and ensure safe traversal.

These mechanisms ensure NDH‑CORE remains safe for:

- neurodivergent users  
- trauma survivors  
- interdisciplinary collaborators  
- children  
- future AI traversal agents  

---

## 6 — Integration Flow

The integration flow is:

1. **Seed curvature** feeds into  
2. **Tile stability**, which feeds into  
3. **Crossmap transitions**, which are guarded by  
4. **Sneks**, all of which are stabilized by  
5. **Weave Harmonics**, which enforce  
6. **TISD global safety principles**

This creates a **closed safety loop**.
