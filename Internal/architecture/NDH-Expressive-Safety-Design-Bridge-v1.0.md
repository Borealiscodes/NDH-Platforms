## **1 — Purpose**

This bridge document establishes the formal relationship between:

- **MRPRS** (expressive‑safety membrane inside Trauma‑Informed‑Systems‑Design)  
- **Upstream expressive‑safety sequencing**  
- **NDH‑Platforms expressive‑safety implementation layer**  

It ensures that expressive‑safety theory and expressive‑safety UI/UX implementation remain structurally aligned across the constellation.

This document is required by the **Sequencing Directive** and must be referenced before any provenance, geometry, or VM‑layer activation.

---

## **2 — Upstream Expressive‑Safety Theory (TISD)**

Expressive‑safety theory lives entirely inside **Trauma‑Informed‑Systems‑Design**, and consists of the following upstream sequence:

### **2.1 MRPRS**  
Defines expressive‑safety invariants, trauma‑safe reconstruction rules, and non‑dual representational boundaries.  
Reference: **MRPRS**

### **2.2 Drift Thresholds**  
Define expressive‑safety drift corridors, collapse buffers, and deviation limits.  
Reference: **Drift Thresholds**

### **2.3 Anchor Protocols**  
Define anchor grammar, reconstruction anchors, and expressive‑safety anchor invariants.  
Reference: **Anchor Protocols**

### **2.4 SCI‑80 Binding Rules**  
Define expressive‑safety diagram grammar, symbolic invariants, and visual anchor binding rules.  
Reference: **SCI‑80**

### **2.5 Constellation Visual Anchor Integration**  
Integrates MRPRS anchors, SCI‑80 grammar, and constellation visual primitives.  
Reference: **Visual Anchor Integration**

### ⭐ **These five steps must be completed before NDH‑Platforms can implement expressive‑safety UI/UX.**

## **3 — Downstream Expressive‑Safety Implementation (NDH‑Platforms)**

NDH‑Platforms implements expressive‑safety principles through UI/UX artifacts.  
These artifacts **do not** define expressive‑safety theory — they **apply** it.
