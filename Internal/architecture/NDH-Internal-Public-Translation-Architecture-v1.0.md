# 🛰️ **NDH Internal → Public Translation Architecture (v1.0)**  
### *How NDH transforms internal reports, companions, and governance outputs into public‑safe artifacts*

**Scope:** Internal — Developer Grade  
**Status:** Next‑Layer Architecture  
**Version:** 1.0

---

## ⭐ 1 — Purpose of the Translation Architecture

NDH now has:

- internal reports  
- internal companions  
- internal telemetry  
- internal governance signals  
- internal search engine  
- internal repo governance  

But NDH also produces **public artifacts**, and those must be:

- safe  
- conceptual  
- non‑leaking  
- non‑drifting  
- epistemically inclusive  
- structurally consistent  
- indexable by the accessible search engine  

The translation architecture defines **how internal → public transformation happens**.

---

## ⭐ 2 — The Translation Pipeline (High‑Level)

NDH translation follows a **four‑stage pipeline**:

1. **Internal Artifact Selection**  
2. **Sanitization & Redaction**  
3. **Public Reconstruction**  
4. **Accessible Search Engine Indexing**

This pipeline ensures that:

- internal mechanics stay internal  
- public artifacts remain meaningful  
- epistemic lenses remain user‑controlled  
- governance signals never leak  
- search engines stay aligned  

---

## ⭐ 3 — Stage 1: Internal Artifact Selection

Internal artifacts eligible for translation:

- **Emergent Case Study (Internal)**  
- **Meta Analysis (Internal)**  
- **Meta‑Meta Analysis (Internal)**  
- **Research Companion (Internal)**  
- **Philosophy Companion (Internal)**  
- **Ethics Companion (Internal)**  

Artifacts *not* eligible:

- GQL Processing Specification  
- Raw Dataset Population Plan  
- Repo Integration Checklist  
- Telemetry schemas  
- Governance math  
- NDH invariants  
- NDH manifold semantics  

These remain internal only.

---

## ⭐ 4 — Stage 2: Sanitization & Redaction

Sanitization removes:

- raw telemetry  
- governance signals  
- drift metrics  
- compliance posture  
- stability indicators  
- access anomalies  
- internal invariants  
- NDH manifold semantics  
- GQL evaluation logic  
- internal repo paths  
- internal provenance footers  

Redaction rules:

- replace governance signals with conceptual summaries  
- replace telemetry with narrative examples  
- replace invariants with high‑level principles  
- replace internal diagrams with public‑safe diagrams  
- remove any reference to internal layers  

This ensures **no leakage**.

---

## ⭐ 5 — Stage 3: Public Reconstruction

Public artifacts must be:

- conceptual  
- narrative  
- diagram‑friendly  
- epistemically inclusive  
- safe for external consumption  
- accessible to non‑technical readers  
- indexable by the accessible search engine  

Public reconstruction uses:

- high‑level explanations  
- analogies  
- diagrams  
- conceptual metaphors  
- ethics/philosophy framing  
- accessibility/snek modes  
- AI‑Zen modes  

Public versions are generated via:

- **Emergent Case Study (Public)**  
- **Meta Analysis (Public)**  
- **Meta‑Meta Analysis (Public)**  
- **AI‑Zen Companion**  
- **Accessibility/Snek Companion**  

These are the public‑safe outputs.

---

## ⭐ 6 — Stage 4: Accessible Search Engine Indexing

Public artifacts are indexed by:

- type  
- lens  
- topic  
- conceptual domain  
- diagram type  
- narrative mode  
- accessibility mode  

The accessible search engine uses:

- gentle language  
- inclusive framing  
- non‑technical metaphors  
- non‑directive lens suggestions  

It never indexes:

- internal mechanics  
- telemetry  
- GQL internals  
- governance signals  
- invariants  
- repo governance rules  

This preserves NDH’s safety boundary.

---

## ⭐ 7 — Translation Architecture Summary (Zen‑Clean)

> **Internal → sanitize → reconstruct → index → public.**  
>  
> Internal artifacts contain truth.  
> Public artifacts contain meaning.  
>  
> GQL stays internal.  
> Telemetry stays internal.  
> Governance math stays internal.  
>  
> Public artifacts are conceptual, safe, and epistemically inclusive.  
>  
> This architecture ensures NDH can communicate externally without leaking internally.
