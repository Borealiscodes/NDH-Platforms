# **🌍 Major Satellite Imagery Providers for Public & Humanitarian Use**





---

## **🛰️ Public / Government Providers (Free or Open Access)**

- **NASA Worldview** — Near‑real‑time global imagery updated within hours; 1,200+ layers; disaster monitoring; animations; historical archive.  
    [Earthdata](https://www.earthdata.nasa.gov/data/tools/worldview)

- **NASA LANCE** — Near‑real‑time (3–5 hour latency) fire, flood, weather, and hazard monitoring.  
    [Earthdata](https://www.earthdata.nasa.gov/data/projects/lance)

- **NOAA GOES** — Real‑time geostationary weather imagery updated continuously; widely used for hurricanes, storms, and humanitarian response.  
    [ts2.tech](https://ts2.tech/en/live-satellite-images-and-real-time-maps-top-platforms-for-web-mobile/)

- **ESA Copernicus Sentinel (EO Browser)** — Free access to Sentinel‑1 (SAR), Sentinel‑2 (optical), Sentinel‑3 (environmental), with band combinations, NDVI, time‑lapse, and 3D terrain.  
    [ts2.tech](https://ts2.tech/en/live-satellite-images-and-real-time-maps-top-platforms-for-web-mobile/)

- **NASA GIBS** — Full‑resolution global imagery accessible via WMTS/WMS APIs; near‑real‑time layers updated within hours.  
    [Earthdata](https://www.earthdata.nasa.gov/engage/open-data-services-software/earthdata-developer-portal/gibs-api)

---

## **🛰️ Aggregators / Public Viewers (Free)**

- **Zoom Earth** — Aggregates NOAA, EUMETSAT, JMA Himawari, NASA MODIS; updates every 10–15 minutes; widely used in humanitarian response.  
    [ts2.tech](https://ts2.tech/en/live-satellite-images-and-real-time-maps-top-platforms-for-web-mobile/)

- **Google Earth** — High‑resolution imagery (Landsat + aerial); not real‑time but historically rich; humanitarian teams use it for baseline comparison.  
    [ts2.tech](https://ts2.tech/en/live-satellite-images-and-real-time-maps-top-platforms-for-web-mobile/)

---

## **🛰️ Commercial Providers (Humanitarian Programs + Paid Access)**

- **Planet Labs** — Daily global coverage (3–4 m) + high‑res SkySat (50 cm); NICFI program provides free tropical forest monitoring for public interest.  
    [ts2.tech](https://ts2.tech/en/live-satellite-images-and-real-time-maps-top-platforms-for-web-mobile/)

- **Maxar SecureWatch** — 30 cm resolution; massive archive; ~3 million km² new imagery daily; delivery within 48 hours; used heavily in humanitarian mapping.  
    [ts2.tech](https://ts2.tech/en/live-satellite-images-and-real-time-maps-top-platforms-for-web-mobile/)

- **Airbus OneAtlas** — Pléiades Neo (30 cm), SPOT, TerraSAR‑X; near‑real‑time tasking; optical + radar; 30‑day free trial.  
    [ts2.tech](https://ts2.tech/en/live-satellite-images-and-real-time-maps-top-platforms-for-web-mobile/)

- **Capella Space** — High‑resolution SAR (50 cm); all‑weather, day/night; Open Data program for humanitarian use.  
    [ts2.tech](https://ts2.tech/en/live-satellite-images-and-real-time-maps-top-platforms-for-web-mobile/)

- **EOS LandViewer** — Aggregates Sentinel, Landsat, MODIS + commercial high‑res; NDVI, change detection; freemium model.  
    [ts2.tech](https://ts2.tech/en/live-satellite-images-and-real-time-maps-top-platforms-for-web-mobile/)
