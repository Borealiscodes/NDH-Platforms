# **✨ Constellation Activation Diagram v1.0**  
### *NDH‑Platforms → Ontology → BlueprintSuite*

A governed activation model describing how NDH’s expressive and ontology layers “light up” together as a single constellation once all PNGs, guardians, and safety envelope semantics are active.

---

## **1 — Purpose**

The Constellation Activation Diagram shows how:

- traversal PNGs  
- public PNGs  
- ontology nodes  
- guardians  
- safety envelope semantics  

form a unified, reversible, trauma‑informed constellation.

---

## **2 — Constellation Components**

### **Traversal Stars**
- Safety Envelope  
- Orbital Rings  
- Guardian Roles  
- Trans‑Orbital Flow  

### **Public Stars**
- Public Interaction Flow  
- Soft Mistake Handling  
- Habitat Map  

### **Binding Lines**
- Ontology nodes  
- Guardian semantics  
- Safety Envelope perimeter  

---

## **3 — Activation Conditions**

The constellation activates only when:

- All 7 PNGs exist at **v1.0**  
- PNG Integration Blueprint is stable  
- NDH Search Engine Ontology is finalized  
- Enterprise Docking Analysis is complete  

---

## **4 — Activation Sequence**

1. Render Specs online  
2. PNG Integration Blueprint online  
3. All PNGs rendered  
4. Ontology finalized  
5. Docking Analysis complete  
6. Constellation activation  

---

## **5 — Constellation Semantics**

- **Non‑hierarchical** — no star is central  
- **Reversible** — all paths reversible  
- **Non‑evaluative** — no ranking or scoring  
- **Trauma‑informed** — safety envelope governs all motion
